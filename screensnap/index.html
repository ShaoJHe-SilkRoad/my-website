ScreenSnap

One click, or Alt+C, and your whole screen is on the clipboard.

Download for Windows · v0.1.0

Windows 10 / 11 · portable ZIP · unsigned · uses the built-in Windows PowerShell, nothing to install

Get started

  1. Download the ZIP. Before extracting it, right-click it, choose Properties, tick Unblock and click OK. Windows marks downloaded scripts, and many work PCs will not run them until they are unblocked.
  2. Extract it to a folder you will keep, for example Documents\ScreenSnap.
  3. Double-click ScreenSnap.vbs. The lens appears near the top-right of your screen.

Use it

  • Click the lens or press Alt+C in any app. Every monitor is captured and the image goes on your clipboard. Paste it with Ctrl+V.
  • The lens hides during the capture, so it never appears in your screenshots. A white flash and a green check mean the image is ready; red means the capture failed.
  • Clicking the lens does not take focus from the app you are using, so you can paste straight away.
  • Drag the lens to move it. Right-click it for Take screenshot or Exit.

Nothing is saved to disk or sent anywhere: the screenshot only goes to your clipboard. It includes everything visible on your screen, so check what is showing before you paste it somewhere.

Start it with Windows

Press Win+R, type shell:startup and press Enter. Put a shortcut to ScreenSnap.vbs in the folder that opens.

Common problems

  • Nothing happens when I open ScreenSnap.vbs: the files are probably still blocked. Delete the extracted folder, unblock the ZIP as in step 1, and extract it again. If your organization blocks VBScript, start it with:
    powershell -NoProfile -STA -WindowStyle Hidden -File "C:\path\to\ScreenSnap.ps1"
  • Alt+C does nothing: another app already uses Alt+C, and the ALT+C label on the lens is hidden. Clicking still works. While ScreenSnap runs, Alt+C belongs to it in every app; exit ScreenSnap to give it back.
  • Opening it again does nothing: only one copy runs at a time. Look for the lens on screen; it may be behind a notification.

ScreenSnap

点一下，或按 Alt+C，整个屏幕就进了剪贴板。

下载 Windows 版 · v0.1.0

Windows 10 / 11 · 免安装 ZIP · 未签名 · 使用系统自带的 Windows PowerShell，无需安装其他软件

开始使用

  1. 下载 ZIP。解压之前，右键点击它，选择“属性”，勾选“解除锁定”，再点“确定”。Windows 会标记下载的脚本，很多工作电脑在解除锁定前不会运行它们。
  2. 解压到一个长期保留的文件夹，例如 文档\ScreenSnap。
  3. 双击 ScreenSnap.vbs，镜头会出现在屏幕右上方附近。

怎么用

  • 在任何应用中点击镜头或按 Alt+C，所有显示器的画面都会被截取并放进剪贴板，用 Ctrl+V 粘贴即可。
  • 截图时镜头会自动隐藏，不会出现在截图里。白光一闪并出现绿色对勾，表示截图已就绪；红色表示截图失败。
  • 点击镜头不会抢走当前应用的焦点，可以直接粘贴。
  • 拖动镜头可以移动位置，右键可选择“截图”或“退出”。

截图不会保存到硬盘，也不会发送到任何地方，只会放进剪贴板。截图包含屏幕上所有可见内容，粘贴前请留意屏幕上显示了什么。

开机自动启动

按 Win+R，输入 shell:startup 并回车，把 ScreenSnap.vbs 的快捷方式放进打开的文件夹。

常见问题

  • 打开 ScreenSnap.vbs 没有反应：文件很可能仍被锁定。删除已解压的文件夹，按第 1 步解除 ZIP 的锁定后重新解压。如果单位禁用了 VBScript，可以用以下命令启动：
    powershell -NoProfile -STA -WindowStyle Hidden -File "C:\path\to\ScreenSnap.ps1"
  • 按 Alt+C 没有反应：另一个应用已经占用了 Alt+C，此时镜头上的 ALT+C 字样会隐藏，点击镜头仍然可用。ScreenSnap 运行期间，Alt+C 在所有应用中都归它使用；退出 ScreenSnap 即可恢复。
  • 再次打开没有反应：同一时间只运行一个副本。请在屏幕上找找镜头，它可能被通知挡住了。
