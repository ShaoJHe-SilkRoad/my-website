ScreenSnap

Screenshot lens

One click. Whole screen. On your clipboard.

A small floating lens that copies a screenshot of every monitor to your clipboard. Click it or press Alt+C in any app, then paste.

  • Windows 10 / 11
  • Alt+C shortcut
  • Nothing to install
Get ScreenSnap↗
Behind the project+
What it is for
Getting what is on your screen into a message or document in one motion, without saving files.
How to start
Download the ZIP from the ScreenSnap page, unblock it, extract it and open ScreenSnap.vbs. The lens appears near the top-right of your screen.
Good to know
It uses the built-in Windows PowerShell, never saves or sends your screenshots, and hides itself so it is never in the picture.
