AUV

Agent Usage Viewer

Know how much AI coding time you have left.

AUV is a desktop app for Mac and Windows that shows your Codex and Claude usage and limits at a glance, so you can pace your work. It also includes ThreadOS, for preparing instructions for a team of coding agents, and on Mac, Sectorium, for managing a development drive.

Behind the project+
What it is for
One app for account usage, team instructions and Mac development volumes.
The hardest parts
Exact Claude usage comes from a signed-in Safari session, so AUV has to keep reading it when Safari is hidden, minimized or on another desktop. The other half of the work is shipping a signed, notarized Universal Mac app alongside a separate Windows version.
Where help is needed
Windows is the gap: it is still at 0.16.3, ships unsigned, and lacks Sectorium’s storage actions. A developer who knows Windows packaging and code signing would make the biggest difference. Testing and design feedback from people who use Codex or Claude every day is just as welcome.
